# Soundboard v1.3.0

Soundboard v1.3.0 keeps your physical microphone audible while sounds play and
adds a real pause for playback.

## Highlights

- **Voice Priority**: automatic microphone-triggered ducking that lowers sounds
  while you speak. Only the selected physical microphone branch is analysed,
  before it is mixed with soundboard playback, so soundboard audio can never
  trigger its own ducking.
- Voice sensitivity choices of Low (-30 dBFS), Normal (-36 dBFS), and High
  (-42 dBFS), with separate engage and release thresholds so the state does not
  toggle near the threshold.
- Ducking strength choices of Light (-6 dB), Balanced (-12 dB), and Strong
  (-18 dB). Sounds fade down over 50 ms, stay lowered for at least 200 ms, and
  return over 500 ms.
- The microphone keeps its existing unity application gain. The sound-only
  monitor is lowered by the same amount, so headphones hear a representative
  result.
- **Pause and resume playback** from the main window. Paused sounds stop
  advancing through their source data and resume from their exact previous
  sample position, with trim, fades, per-sound volume, master volume, and
  concurrent sessions unchanged.
- Optional global **Pause/Resume playback** hotkey with the existing bare-key
  support, modifier combinations, duplicate and conflict validation, and
  persistence. No shortcut is assigned by default.
- Microphone passthrough, the audio service, and device connections keep
  running while playback is paused.

## Defaults and compatibility

Voice Priority is opt-in and off after an update, so existing installations
keep their previous audio behavior until the toggle is switched on. Its
defaults are Normal sensitivity and Balanced strength.

Sounds, categories, ordering, favorites, hotkeys, trim and fade settings,
per-sound volumes, master volume, device selections, and monitoring settings
are unchanged and load without a settings schema change. VB-CABLE routing,
concurrent playback, retriggering behavior, one-shot playback, device
recovery, boundary clamping, and the absence of normalization or automatic
microphone gain are all unchanged.

Soundboard still does not change Windows volume-mixer or endpoint volume,
microphone hardware gain, communication-session ducking, VB-CABLE, Discord, or
game settings.

## Installation options

- `Soundboard-Setup-v1.3.0-win-x64.exe` is the per-user installer. Its normal
  destination is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Soundboard`.
- `Soundboard-v1.3.0-win-x64-portable.zip` is the self-contained portable
  release.

Both options preserve user settings, the sound library, hotkeys, and managed
audio under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Soundboard`.

## Validation

The Release x64 application build completed with zero warnings and errors, and
package verification checked the release versions, artifact contents, hashes,
architecture, dependency versions, privacy boundaries, and installer scope.

Functional verification of this release is outstanding. Live microphone
detection, ducking behavior in a game or Discord, pause and resume during real
playback, hotkey registration on the user's system, and audible routing were
not tested and require the user's own hardware and applications.
